Filing a health insurance claim in Florida

Most of the time, your doctor or hospital files the claim for you. This guide covers what to do when you need to file one yourself, or when a claim gets denied.

Before you start: find your insurance card, the bill or itemized statement, and your Explanation of Benefits (EOB) if you have one.

Step-by-step

  1. Check whether the provider already filed

    Call your provider's billing office or check your insurer's member portal. If they already filed, you may not need to do anything.

  2. Get the claim form

    Download it from your insurer's website or call the member services number on your card. [PLACEHOLDER: add tips for common Florida insurers]

  3. Gather supporting documents

    Itemized bill, receipts, referral or prior authorization (if required), and any medical records requested.

  4. Submit before the deadline

    Most plans set a filing deadline. [PLACEHOLDER: typical deadline ranges, verify]

  5. Review your Explanation of Benefits

    The EOB shows what was paid, what was denied, and why. It is not a bill.

  6. Appeal if denied

    You have the right to an internal appeal and, in many cases, an independent external review. [PLACEHOLDER: Florida appeal timelines and process]

What to keep

  • Insurance card (front and back)
  • Itemized bills and receipts
  • Explanation of Benefits (EOB) statements
  • Claim number and dates of every call
  • Copies of everything you send
