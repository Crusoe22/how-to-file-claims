Filing an auto insurance claim in Florida

Florida is a "no-fault" state. Your own Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage generally pays first for your injuries, no matter who caused the crash.

Safety first: if anyone is hurt, call 911. Get medical care promptly, because Florida PIP has time limits for seeking treatment. [PLACEHOLDER: verify PIP treatment deadline]

Step-by-step

  1. At the scene

    Move the vehicle to safety, attempt to call the police and exchange names, insurance, and plate numbers with the other drivers. If your vehicle is towed wrie down the tow companies name and contact information. [PLACEHOLDER: when Florida requires a police report]

  2. Document the scene

    Photograph all vehicles involved and the location where the accident occurred. If possible obtain witness contact information.

  3. Notify your insurer

    Report the accident as soon as possible and write down your claim number. Find your insurance company's claims contact information.

  4. Get your crash report

    [PLACEHOLDER: how to request a Florida crash report and the agency link]

  5. Work with the adjuster

    An adjuster will review the damage. You can get your own repair estimates.

  6. Review the settlement

    Make sure the offer covers repairs, medical costs, and other losses before you accept. If you disagree, ask for the explanation in writing.
