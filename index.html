The basics that apply to every claim

  1. Read your policy

    Know what's covered, your deductible, and any deadlines for reporting a loss.

  2. Report the claim promptly

    Contact your insurer as soon as possible. Write down your claim number and the name of everyone you speak with.

  3. Document everything

    Keep photos, receipts, bills, reports, and copies of every letter and email.

  4. Follow up and know your options

    If your claim is delayed or denied, you can appeal, and Florida has free state help available. See resources.
